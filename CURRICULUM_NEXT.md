# First Principles — Curriculum Expansion

The first 10 lessons are the onboarding adventure, not the curriculum. New sets should feel like new game worlds while revisiting earlier ideas in richer contexts.

## Design constraints
- Interaction before explanation.
- No adjacent lessons should rely on the same primary interaction.
- Every discovery has an observable transfer challenge.
- Introduce advanced intuition before formal notation.
- Mix mathematical knowledge with CogAT-style reasoning skills.
- New mastery should create new activity/orders in My World.
- Keep each lesson approximately 2–4 minutes.

## Adventure 2 — Relationships & Scaling (Lessons 11–20)
11. **Scale the Robot** — drag a size slider while keeping body proportions. Concept: proportional scaling. Mechanic: linked sliders. World: Pattern Studio design order.
12. **Potion Recipe** — drag ingredients into a 3:2 mixture. Concept: ratios. Mechanic: drag/drop recipe.
13. **Same Amount, New Language** — morph 1/4 among fraction, decimal, percent, ratio, money and time. Mechanic: transformation/match wheel.
14. **Resize the Map** — stretch a map and predict doubled distances. Concept: scale factor. Mechanic: pinch/slider.
15. **Race Pace** — compare two cars by distance per unit time. Concept: rate. Mechanic: animated race controls.
16. **Mystery Machine** — feed numbers into a machine and infer its rule. Concept: functions. Mechanic: input/output machine.
17. **Build the Rule** — arrange operation blocks to reproduce a function. Concept: algebraic rules. Mechanic: block sequencing.
18. **Slope Mountain** — change rise/run and watch a path tilt. Concept: slope as ratio. Mechanic: two-axis sliders.
19. **Which Deal Wins?** — compare unit prices visually. Concept: unit rate. Mechanic: shopping/scale comparison.
20. **Relationship Mission** — mixed transfer mission using ratios, rates, scale and functions. Mechanic: multi-stage challenge.

## Adventure 3 — Geometry & Space (Lessons 21–30)
21. **Shape Builder** — drag sides/vertices to create constrained shapes. Concept: geometric properties.
22. **Same Area, Different Fence** — build two parks with equal area and different perimeter. Mechanic: tile construction.
23. **Triangle Factory** — split rectangles and recombine triangles. Concept: area intuition.
24. **Angle Launcher** — rotate a launcher to hit targets at named/unnamed angles. Mechanic: rotation + launch.
25. **Mirror Lab** — drag a mirror line and predict reflections. Concept: symmetry.
26. **Turn the World** — rotate/translate/reflect shapes on a grid. Concept: transformations.
27. **Treasure Coordinates** — navigate negative and positive coordinates. Mechanic: map/pathfinding.
28. **Shortest Route** — compare paths and discover diagonal distance intuition. Concept: distance/Pythagorean precursor.
29. **Build a 3D Room** — assemble nets/cubes and reason about views. CogAT: spatial transformation.
30. **Architect Mission** — satisfy area, perimeter, symmetry and coordinate constraints in one build.

## Adventure 4 — Chance, Patterns & Logic (Lessons 31–40)
31. **Mystery Bag** — sample colored objects and predict likelihood. Concept: probability.
32. **Fair or Unfair?** — modify a spinner to make outcomes fair. Mechanic: spinner construction.
33. **How Many Ways?** — physically arrange outfits/paths. Concept: combinatorics.
34. **Pattern Portal** — complete visual matrices. CogAT: figure matrices.
35. **Number Train** — infer increasingly complex number-series rules. CogAT: number series.
36. **Analogy Machine** — discover A:B :: C:? relationships. CogAT: analogies.
37. **Logic Gates** — route signals through AND/OR-style switches without formal notation first. Concept: Boolean reasoning.
38. **Constraint Builder** — construct an object satisfying several rules simultaneously.
39. **Prediction vs Surprise** — compare expected and observed outcomes over repeated trials.
40. **Carnival Mission** — probability/logic/combinatorics transfer challenge; unlocks a carnival district.

## Adventure 5 — Motion, Forces & Change (Lessons 41–50)
41. **Speed Track** — manipulate distance and time, observe speed.
42. **Vector Spaceship** — combine horizontal and vertical pushes to reach a target.
43. **Momentum Crash Lab** — vary mass and speed, observe collision effects; introduce p = mv only after intuition.
44. **Balance the Bridge** — place weights at distances around a pivot. Concept: torque.
45. **Roller Coaster Energy** — change hill height and observe speed. Concept: potential/kinetic energy.
46. **Gravity Worlds** — launch the same object under different gravity strengths.
47. **Orbit Puzzle** — tune speed/direction to keep a satellite near a planet. Concept: orbital intuition.
48. **Change Machine** — watch a graph grow as a quantity changes. Concept: rate of change.
49. **Area From Slices** — fill curved regions with increasingly thin strips. Concept: integration intuition.
50. **Spaceport Mission** — combine coordinates, vectors, ratios, motion and gravity; unlock the Spaceport landmark.

## Later frontier
- Exponential growth through doubling games.
- Infinity through zooming, subdivision and never-ending patterns.
- Limits through approaching a target.
- Derivatives as instantaneous speed/change.
- Networks and shortest paths.
- Optimization under constraints.
- Binary and information.
- Simple circuits and computation.
- Statistics, distributions and uncertainty.

## World connection
The city should react to these arcs. Ratios/rates add Bakery and shop orders; geometry adds Architecture Studio projects; probability opens Carnival orders; motion/forces open Transit, Bridge Works and amusement-park projects; coordinates/vectors expand Transit and Spaceport missions. Returning after learning should reveal new pending orders, visual upgrades and eventually new districts rather than merely replaying old questions.
